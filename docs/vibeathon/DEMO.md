# Two-minute demo (skeleton)

1. (0:00) Bluehost landing: "Buying one outfit from five stores, you never see the whole look." Open Hrishikesh's profile.
2. (0:15) Stage page: avatar at the centre, merchant shops around it. Flip Kaapi Club tees, Rao & Sons blazers on the avatar.
3. (0:40) Prompt: "baggy trousers, a red cap and a white shirt, I am presenting on stage." The stylist applies it slot by slot with notes per merchant.
4. (1:05) Go on stage: Showtime lights, LED wall says HRISHIKESH, camera wide, audience, close. Stage check: score, three notes, one-tap swap.
5. (1:30) Add look to bag: one bag, four merchants. Checkout as an order request; order email arrives.
6. (1:45) Merchant portal: Rao & Sons sees its share of the order. Close.

## Assets

- Stage URL (full screen backup), Bluehost app URL, /brand/hero.jpg as the WebGL fallback.
- Gmail open on the demo phone or laptop for the order email.
